Explore mode's open question was the balance of visual against information focus. The iteration narrows to the window family: three variants of a floating reading window over the same seamless roll (EB01, columns 44–48), each with a different sidebar system.

Variant Mechanism
Window-dual Dual sidebars flanking the roll; the window couples to either side — dock a window to a sidebar, tear a sidebar section out as a window.
Window-styles One window, four chrome styles — frosted, card, docked, tabbed — plus a second pinned window for a held passage.
Window-split Split right sidebar: reading pane above, secondary apparatus panel below, each collapsible independently.
