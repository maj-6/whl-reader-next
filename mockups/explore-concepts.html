Explore mode's open question is the balance of visual against information focus. Five mechanisms surface the same text on the same seamless roll (EB01, columns 44–48); each sits at a different point on that spectrum.

Concept Mechanism VisualInformation
Window Movable, resizable reading window floating over the roll; translucency-adjustable.
Ghost Translation as an in-place ghost layer on the papyrus itself; opacity-controlled, no panel chrome.
Apparatus Fixed compact gloss rail synced to the viewport, as in a critical edition's apparatus.
Lens Cursor loupe: hold to peek the translation under the pointer; click freezes a small card.
Ribbon Facing translation ribbon: a second seamless band scrolling in lockstep beneath the roll.
