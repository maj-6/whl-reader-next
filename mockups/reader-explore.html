running translation · fol. 12r
12r · a · entry, rubricated

This herb the Greeks call chamaemelon; the Italians name it the sweet‑smelling, for above all other herbs it yields good odour. Gather this herb before the sun rises, the root washed clean; ground with the seed of chamomile it drives off the white film of the eye.

12r · b · additio, later hand

Pills: take aloes two ounces; myrobalans, mastic and red roses of each one ounce; saffron one drachm; make up with juice of fennel.
