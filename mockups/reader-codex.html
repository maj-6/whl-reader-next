3242蛇床子 shé chuáng zǐ (《本经》)
【异名】蛇米(《本经》),虺床(《尔雅》),蛇珠(《吴普本草》),蛇床仁(《药性论》)。【基原】为伞形科植物蛇床的干燥成熟果实。

ENSHECHUANGZI — fruit of Cnidium monnieri (L.) Cusson; first listed in the Ben jing. Also shé mǐ (Ben jing), huǐ chuáng (Erya), shé zhū (Wu Pu). Source: the dried ripe fruit of she chuang, family Apiaceae.

【性味】辛苦,温。①《本经》:"味苦,平。"②《别录》:"辛甘,无毒。"
【功用主治】温肾助阳,祛风,燥湿,杀虫。治阳痿,宫冷,寒湿带下,湿痹腰痛。

ENNATURE & FLAVOUR — acrid, bitter; warm. Ben jing: "bitter, neutral." Bie lu: "acrid and sweet, non-toxic." ACTIONS — warms the kidney and assists yang; dispels wind, dries dampness, kills parasites. For impotence, cold uterus, damp leukorrhea, lumbar pain from damp obstruction.

【选方】①治妇人阴痒:蛇床子一两,白矾二钱。煎汤频洗。(《濒湖集简方》)

ENSELECTED PRESCRIPTION — for pruritus vulvae: one liǎng of shechuangzi and two qián of alum (bái fán), decocted; applied warm as a frequent wash. (Binhu jijian fang.)

region · queued

【各家论述】《纲目》:"蛇床乃右肾命门、少阳三焦气分之药。《神农》列之上品,不独助男子,而又有益妇人。"

ENCOMMENTARY — Gang mu: "Shechuang is a drug of the gate of life of the right kidney and of the qi aspect of the lesser-yang sān jiāo. The Shen nong ranks it in the top grade: it does not merely assist men — it benefits women too."

Umbellifer of sandy lowlands; the fruit is the drug. Top-grade in the Ben jing; modern work centres on its coumarin osthole.
